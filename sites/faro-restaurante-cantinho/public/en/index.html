Inside

A small room, a fireplace, and Portuguese cooking done the house way.

“What we tried to do was translate and interpret a little of traditional Portuguese cooking — in other words, make it our way.”

Diogo Ferreira, one of the people behind Cantinho, to MAGG magazine (2021, translated from Portuguese)
  • Fish and seafood fresh, from the Algarve coast.
  • The fire is lit in the winter months.
  • A terrace outside, for warm evenings.
  • On the walls, work by young Algarve artists. [CONFIRMAR COM CLIENTE]
