2. What data we process and why

This policy follows Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Portuguese Law 58/2019.

  • Booking requests. The booking form does not send anything to us or to any server: it only writes, on your own device, a message that you then send by WhatsApp or SMS. We receive what you type (name, date, time, number of guests, seating preference and notes) and the phone number or WhatsApp profile you send it from. We use this only to confirm and manage your booking. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Allergies and intolerances. If you tell us about them, we use that health information only to prepare your meal safely, based on your explicit consent when you share it (Article 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time; just tell us at the table.
  • Calls and messages. If you call or write to us, we use your contact details only to reply.
  • Hosting logs. The site's hosting provider (Vercel Inc.) logs technical data for each request, such as IP address, date and time and browser type, to deliver the pages and protect the service against abuse. Legal basis: legitimate interest in keeping the site secure (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

3. How long we keep it

We delete booking messages and allergy information within 30 days of the booking date, unless the law requires us to keep them longer. [CONFIRMAR COM CLIENTE: prazo] Hosting logs are kept by the provider for a short period, under its own policy.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell or hand over personal data. If you send your booking by WhatsApp, the message goes through WhatsApp's service (WhatsApp Ireland Ltd., Meta group) under the terms you accepted with them; by SMS, it goes through your mobile operator and ours. The hosting provider (Vercel Inc., USA) acts as our processor; transfers outside the European Economic Area rely on GDPR safeguards (EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or standard contractual clauses).

5. Cookies and third-party services

This site uses no cookies, stores nothing in your browser, and has no analytics, advertising or social media tools. Fonts and images are served by the site itself, and the map is a static drawing made from OpenStreetMap data: no Google Maps or other third-party content is embedded. That is why we do not ask for cookie consent.

Links to Google Maps, Apple Maps, WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, the Electronic Complaints Book and other sites only open when you click them, and those pages have their own privacy and cookie policies.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, erase, restrict or port your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest and withdraw consent at any time, using the contact details in section 1. We reply within one month. You also have the right to complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD), www.cnpd.pt.
