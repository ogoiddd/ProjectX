2. What this site does (and doesn’t)

  • The site is static: no forms that send data to a server, no accounts, no database.
  • The “table request” only builds a text on your own device. Nothing leaves the site until you tap “Send by WhatsApp”, “By SMS” or “Call” and send the message or place the call from your phone.
  • We use no cookies and no analytics, advertising or embedded social media tools. Fonts and the map are hosted on this site; the map is a drawing made from OpenStreetMap data and contacts no external service.
  • If you switch language, your browser stores the word “en” or “pt” (local storage) to remember your choice. It identifies no one, is strictly necessary for the feature you asked for, and you can clear it in your browser settings.

3. Data we receive when you ask for a table

When you send us the message or call, we receive: your name, the phone number (or WhatsApp account) you send from, the number of people, the requested day and time, any dishes you mention and the note you write.

If your note mentions allergies or intolerances, that is health information. We use it only to prepare your meal safely, based on the explicit consent you give by writing it (GDPR art. 9(2)(a)). You can withdraw that consent at any time by telling us.

4. Why we use it and on what legal basis

  • Handling your booking request (replying, confirming, changing or cancelling): steps taken at your request before a contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Allergies: explicit consent (see point 3).
  • We don’t use your data for advertising and we don’t sell it or share it with third parties.

5. How long we keep it

We delete booking messages within [CONFIRMAR COM CLIENTE: e.g. 30 days] after the booking date, unless we need them longer to deal with a complaint.

6. Who else processes data

  • WhatsApp (WhatsApp Ireland Limited, Meta group): if you choose WhatsApp, the message goes through that service under its own terms. You can always use SMS or phone instead.
  • Telecom operators: for SMS and calls.
  • Website hosting: [CONFIRMAR: Vercel Inc., USA]. Like any web server, it logs technical visit data (IP address, browser, page requested) to deliver the site and protect it against abuse, based on legitimate interest (GDPR art. 6(1)(f)). Transfer to the USA relies on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or the provider’s standard contractual clauses.
  • External links: Google Maps (directions and reviews), Facebook and Tripadvisor only open if you tap the link; from then on their own policies apply.

7. Your rights

You can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction or portability of your data, object to processing and withdraw consent, without affecting anything done before. Just call +351 938 735 167, write to [CONFIRMAR COM CLIENTE: email] or talk to us at the restaurant. We reply within one month.

You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, CNPD: www.cnpd.pt, Av. D. Carlos I, 134, 1.º, 1200-651 Lisboa.

We make no automated decisions and do no profiling.
